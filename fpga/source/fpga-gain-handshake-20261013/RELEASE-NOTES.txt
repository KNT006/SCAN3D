Defect and correction
An AE request coincident with the first periodic startup-register write could
previously receive an acceptance/completion handshake without a 0x3090 gain
write. PCIe register readback therefore did not prove that the sensor gain had
changed. The candidate waits for startup completion and settled AE register
selection, latches the requested gain, and completes after the actual gain
transaction. Periodic startup refresh cannot preempt that transaction.
I2C NACK is retained in the existing error output until the next explicit gain
request. Completion means the bus transaction ended, not unconditional ACK.
